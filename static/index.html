Lockesmith

A research desk for Pokémon Emerald runs

What it explores

Which Pokémon to catch next, what to do next and how to beat the rest of Emerald. Researcher agents check every answer against the game's own data: trainer teams, encounter tables, TMs and the walkthrough.

Who it's for

Players partway through Emerald, including Nuzlocke, custom-rule and randomized runs (upload your randomizer log).

How to use it

  1. Fill in your run on the left, or click an example below.
  2. Ask a question.
  3. Read the cited plan. Open the research trace to see how it got there.

Try an example

One click fills in the question and the run it needs. Then press Ask.
