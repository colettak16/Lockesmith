About LockeSmith

What it explores

Which Pokémon to catch next, what to do next and how to beat the rest of Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed or LeafGreen. Researcher agents check every answer against that game's own data: trainer teams, encounter tables, TMs and the walkthrough. Every claim is cited.

Who it's for

Players partway through Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed or LeafGreen (the Game Boy Advance games), including Nuzlocke, custom-rule and randomized runs (upload your Universal Pokémon Randomizer log).

How to use it

  1. Pick your game and fill in your run on the left, or click one of the examples under the question box.
  2. Ask a question. A catch plan takes about a minute and a half; the researchers' progress shows live.
  3. Read the cited plan. Open the research trace to see how it got there.

Under the hood

A Clarifier reads the question; a Lead sends battle, catch, moves and story researchers out in parallel; a Verifier written in code checks every pick against the data; a Writer cites every claim.

Try:

Data: trainer teams, encounters and TMs from the pret/pokeemerald, pret/pokeruby and pret/pokefirered decompilations; species, moves and learnsets from PokeAPI, corrected to Gen III; walkthrough passages from Bulbapedia's Emerald and Ruby and Sapphire and FireRed and LeafGreen walkthroughs (CC BY-NC-SA 2.5).

Covers the main story of Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed and LeafGreen through the Champion. Doesn't cover other games (including Omega Ruby, Alpha Sapphire and Let's Go), ROM hacks, the postgame (the Battle Frontier, Battle Tower and Sevii Islands 4-7), or exact damage rolls.
