### 1. Sharpedo (or Carvanha)
* **Type:** Water/Dark
* **Why it’s amazing:** Dark-type Pokémon are completely **immune to Psychic-type attacks**, which neutralizes Tate & Liza's primary offensive threat. In Gen 3, all Dark and Water moves are Special, which pairs perfectly with Sharpedo's great Special Attack. You can teach it **Surf** (which hits both opponents) and it learns **Crunch/Bite** naturally to shred their Psychic types.
* **Where to find:** You can catch Carvanha by fishing with a Good Rod on Route 118, or catch Sharpedo directly using the Super Rod (which you can get from a fisherman in Mossdeep City before fighting the Gym). It evolves at level 30.

### 3. Starmie (Staryu)
* **Type:** Water/Psychic
