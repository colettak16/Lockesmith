## Answer

First, a quick clarification for *Pokémon Emerald*: You are about to face **Juan** as the 8th Gym Leader. **Wallace** has been promoted and is now the **Pokémon Champion** at the end of the Elite Four! 

However, because both Juan and Wallace are Water-type masters, your preparation for one will perfectly prepare you for the other. 

Looking at your current team (**Sceptile, Pelipper, Manectric, Breloom**), you already have an **incredibly strong core** against Water types. Sceptile and Manectric alone will absolutely shred most of Juan and Wallace's teams. 

To fill your last two slots and build a team capable of beating Juan and the Elite Four/Wallace, here is what you should catch:

### 1. The Dragon Slayer: Spheal (Evolves into Sealeo -> Walrein)
Right now, your biggest threats are Dragon-types. Juan has a Kingdra (Water/Dragon) that takes neutral damage from Grass and Electric, and the Elite Four has Drake, a Dragon-type master. Because Sceptile and Breloom are incredibly weak to Ice moves, you need an Ice-type of your own.
* **Why:** Walrein is a massive, bulky tank. With the **Ice Beam** TM (found in the Abandoned Ship) and Surf, it will easily solo Drake's dragons, destroy Juan's Kingdra, and stall out Wallace's Milotic. It also resists Ice, making it great against Elite Four Glacia.
* **Where to catch:** Shoal Cave (North of Mossdeep City). Catch a Spheal (levels up to Sealeo at 32, and Walrein at 44). 

### 2. The Elite Four Sweeper: Staryu (Evolves into Starmie)
Pelipper is a great HM user, but Starmie is arguably the best Water-type in Gen 3. 
* **Why:** Starmie is incredibly fast and has massive Special Attack. In Gen 3, you can teach it **Surf, Thunderbolt, Ice Beam, and Psychic**. This combination allows it to hit almost every Pokémon in the game for super-effective damage. It easily handles Wallace's Tentacruel (with Psychic) and Ludicolo (with Ice Beam).
* **Where to catch:** Use the Super Rod (which you can get in Mossdeep City) in Lilycove City to fish up a Staryu. Use a Water Stone to evolve it.

### 3. (Alternative to Starmie) The Ground Immunity: Trapinch (Evolves into Vibrava -> Flygon)
If you don't want two Water-types (Pelipper and Walrein) and want some type variety, get a Ground/Dragon type. 
* **Why:** Manectric is weak to Ground. Wallace and Juan both have a Whiscash (Water/Ground) that is immune to Electric. While Sceptile easily beats Whiscash, having a Pokémon that can use **Earthquake** is vital for dealing with Poison types (like Wallace's Tentacruel) and Steel types in the Elite Four. Flygon is immune to Ground and Electric moves.
* **Where to catch:** The Desert on Route 111 (Catch a Trapinch, evolves at 35 and 45).

### How your team matches up against Wallace (The Champion):
If you bring **Sceptile, Manectric, Breloom, Pelipper, Walrein, and Flygon**, here is how you beat Wallace's team:

* **Wailord:** Manectric (Thunderbolt) or Sceptile (Leaf Blade).
* **Gyarados:** Manectric (Thunderbolt does 4x damage).
* **Whiscash:** Sceptile (Leaf Blade does 4x damage).
* **Ludicolo:** Pelipper (Fly) or Walrein (Ice Beam). Don't use Sceptile or Manectric here, as Ludicolo takes normal damage from them. 
* **Tentacruel:** Flygon (Earthquake). Keep Sceptile and Breloom far away from Tentacruel, as it has Sludge Bomb.
* **Milotic:** This is his ace. It is incredibly tanky and uses Recover, Toxic, and Ice Beam. Hit it hard and fast with Manectric's Thunderbolt or Sceptile's Leaf Blade.

**Next Steps:**
Go catch a **Spheal** in Shoal Cave and grind your team up. For the 8th Gym (Juan), you want your team around **Level 43–45**. By the time you reach Wallace at the end of the Elite Four, you will want your team around **Level 50–55**.
